Every operation records source/sink; rounding at integer boundaries must account residuals. Fixed4000L pond has no implicit rain/evaporation, source fill only on starter/paid expansion. Incoming oxygen, outgoing oxygen/TAN and volume stay auditable. No crop-fertilizer output.

Capacity reserves maximum future mass for every fish:12fish x500g=6000g per4000L pond. Stocking checks reserved mass as well as count/actual biomass; normal growth caps500g and can never invalidate capacity, delete fish or reject a tick. Stage is derived from actual mass and grow-out elapsed ticks; no growth from clicking/frames. Stable IDs never reused, bounded cohort/lot/history arrays. ## Paid biofilter (M5-F02)

After the first chapter,100 Coins installs one filter per pond without cleaning water or providing credit. The pump follows that pond's aerator switch: it operates only after successful aeration in the stable per-pond spending order. TAN conversion is a distinct tracked compartment sink, never a claim that all nitrogen disappears. It adds no water and removes no sludge. Background conversion and effluent remain separate. This fictional balance will be evaluated with final economy policies. An original SVG filter appears only when actually installed; bilingual Water controls show cost, operating dependency and unpaid/waiting state.
